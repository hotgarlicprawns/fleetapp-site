The parts that make it easier to run many agents

Press ⌘J and you jump to the next pane that needs you.

Who needs you

WAITING when an agent asks a question or wants permission. DONE when it finishes. A sound you choose, a Dock count, and a Mac notification if you want one. The states come from Claude Code's own hooks, not from guessing at the screen.

Chats that come back

Each pane resumes its own chat by id when you reopen Fleet. "Restart to update" becomes one button that keeps the chat. Hide the window and your agents keep working in the menu bar.

Bill X-ray, free

Reads the transcripts already on your Mac and shows where your usage went: long chats, cold resumes, model switches, cache breaks, and helpers. Costs are at API list prices, so on a plan they show usage, not your bill.

Cost, context and limits

Each Claude pane shows its cost and how full its context is. The top bar shows your 5-hour and weekly limits for each account. Run several Claude and Codex accounts side by side.

The small things

Clean copy removes the left margin and trailing spaces from what you copy. Clipboard history in the top bar. Drag in images. A mic button for Claude Code's voice mode. Git worktree screens.

Before you trust it

What Fleet touches

  • It runs the official claude and codex commands. It does not modify them or read your login tokens.
  • Your usage stays between you and your vendor. Fleet is not in that path.
  • The X-ray runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no analytics.
  • Fleet itself makes two kinds of network call: a license check after you buy, and a plain request for version.json to see if an update exists. Watch them with Little Snitch if you like.

What it does not do

  • Mac only. No Linux or Windows. Apple Silicon only for now.
  • No remote access from your phone or another machine.
  • If you quit Fleet, its agents stop. Chats resume by id, but a task in progress does not.
  • Codex panes show DONE, not WAITING, and have no per-pane cost.
  • No automatic updates. Fleet tells you when a new version exists, and you download it.
  • Beta, from one developer. Expect bugs and tell me about them.

Install

Paste this in Terminal. It downloads the app, checks its SHA-256, and puts it in Applications. Read the script first.

curl -fsSL https://fleetapp.in/install.sh | sh

Or download the DMG. Until the notarized build ships, macOS warns on first open: go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, then choose Open Anyway.

Questions

Does it work with my Claude plan?

Yes. It runs Claude Code in your own account, the same as a terminal. You sign in through Claude Code, not through Fleet.

Why not tmux or plain terminal tabs?

If those work for you, keep them. Fleet is for when you run enough agents that finding the one waiting for you has become the job.

Will it save me tokens?

We do not promise that. See the table above. The X-ray can show you where your usage goes, and what you change is up to you.

Is this made by Anthropic or OpenAI?

No. Fleet is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by either company. Claude Code and Codex belong to their owners.
